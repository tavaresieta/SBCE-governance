Brazilian Emissions Trading System (SBCE)

Institutional governance and the phased sectoral coverage of the Brazilian MRV Program (PB-MRV).

Last updated: 30 September 2026.

01 · Governance Institutional Governance Structure

Interministerial Committee on Climate Change – CIM

Guidelines for the SBCE, approval of the National Allocation Plan (PNA), conditions for the authorization of ITMOs, among others.

Managing Body

Definition of regulated entities; proposal of the NAPs; accreditation of methodologies for generating CRVEs; definition of CRVE limits in periodic cycles; definition of MRV requirements and procedures; management of the SBCE Central Registry, among others.

Permanent Technical Advisory Committee

Recommendations on NAP criteria, accreditation of methodologies for generating offsets, and allocation of resources from SBCE revenues.

Technical Working Groups (GTs)

(1) Accreditation and De-accreditation of Methodologies for CRVEs Generation; (2) MRV of Emissions; (3) Financial Aspects of the SBCE (kick-off 17 Aug 2026); (4) Industrial Competitiveness — imports and revenue recycling (in formation).

Regulatory Affairs Chambers (CAREGs)

Composed of representative entities from regulated sectors. Consultation on the PNA, emissions cap, MRV, and others.

Designated National Authority

Authorization for the international transfer of mitigation outcomes (ITMOs) for the purposes of Article 6 of the Paris Agreement.

National REDD+ Commission – CONAREDD+ (now under DIMER)

Criteria for accrediting methodologies for generating offsets; information on REDD+ projects and programs; avoidance of double counting.

Securities and Exchange Commission (CVM)

Responsible for regulating the trading of SBCE assets on financial and capital markets. CVM is also granted powers to regulate the use of financial products other than SBCE securities.

National Agency of Petroleum, Natural Gas and Biofuels (ANP)

Responsible for specifying biofuels and regulating all CCS activities, classifiable as emission reductions or removals and potential carbon sinks under the SBCE. All CCS stages require ANP authorization.

Information flows and collaboration between entities

ITMO authorization — proposal and industry response. The draft CIM ITMO Resolution, publicly consulted through 6 Aug 2026, proposed a 50 MtCO2e ceiling for 2031–2035 and a maximum 50% authorization per project/programme. IETA and other private-sector entities (CCS Brasil, ABREMA, IBÁ) are finalizing a joint position — due for publication in the first half of October 2026 — that instead treats the 50 MtCO2e as a floor, drops the single percentage cap (to be set later via ATFs, Resolutions and public calls), allows immediate authorization of mitigation-outcome vintages from 2021 onward, and proposes an Authorisation Reserve for Strategic Activities. Registration of ITMOs as CRVEs in the Central Registry remains solely an administrative/accounting step. Government and industry discuss these points — and methodology-accreditation criteria — on 7 Oct 2026 in Brasília (SEMC Sub-secretariat for Regulation and Methodologies). Everything CRVE/ITMO-related will ultimately sit under the forthcoming Programa Brasileiro de Compensação de Emissões (PBCE); its decree is being finalized for presentation to the GTT in the second half of October 2026, ahead of public consultation.
